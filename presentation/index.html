Overview: The Model, Its Results and Its Limits

What the model does, how well it does it, where the data comes from and what it cannot tell you.
OVERVIEWMAPSDECIDE
Method
A multinomial logistic regression weighs state, lender and data era together on 2,901 datapoints, with 5-fold cross-validation and 150 refits per state to test how stable each leader is.
Result
Lift: 13% lower log loss than national shares; accuracy 55% versus 50% (KPI 8).
Cross-check: 193 (rows checked against outside data), 0 (contradicted) (KPI 9).
Close calls: 24 (states within 20 points) get a forecast; 4 are toss-ups (KPI 4).
Data and Updates
What it answers: which of the three reports a lender will check in each state, and how sure the data is; the funding sequence is built on that forecast.
Sources: a 355-row inquiry table from 16 states plus 670 outside datapoints from 56 cited sources: Doctor of Credit, the myFICO state grid, 2025 to 2026 forum posts and lender pages.
As of: October 7, 2026. Next update: January 7, 2027.
Updates: lender minimums are reviewed every quarter; each new datapoint reruns the cleaning, maps and model from the same scripts.
Data Limits
Consumer-reported: no lender publishes its bureau by state.
Uneven in age: most pulls are from 2008 to 2016; the newest are 2025 and 2026.
Thin states: under 10 pulls is a hint, not a finding.
Not a promise: income, utilization and recent inquiries decide approvals. Not financial advice.

In Scope
Bureau pulls: Experian, Equifax and TransUnion, every state and DC.
Funding types: cards, lines, loans, SBA, auto, equipment and real estate.

Advice: nothing here is legal, tax or investment advice.
Who It Serves
Clients: personal and business credit clients.
Firms: credit consultants, funding companies, advisors and brokers.
Reviewers: analysts and partners tracing each number.

Recommendations: The Best Move for Each Stakeholder

Best move, when to choose it and the next move, for five stakeholder types.
OVERVIEWMAPSDECIDE
Recommendations
Approvals That Add Up
Personal Credit Clients
Best move: Pull all three reports, then apply where your strongest report gets pulled in your state.
Choose this when: one report is clearly stronger or cleaner.
Next move: check your lender on Tableau card 5; prequalify first.
Business Credit Clients
Best move: Treat business card applications as personal inquiries.
Choose this when: you apply for business cards as an owner.
Next move: build the business file in parallel; review the 47 (low or no doc) lenders.
Credit Consulting Companies
Best move: Plan disputes and inquiry spacing bureau by bureau.
Choose this when: a client's three reports disagree or carry errors.
Next move: fix the report target lenders pull first, then apply.
Business Funding Companies
Best move: Apply in strictest-rule order; spread each round across bureaus.
Choose this when: a client is at 700+ (credit score).
Next move: use the funding ladder and one lender per bureau group per round.
Funding Advisors and Brokers
Best move: Run the close-call check before advising.
Choose this when: the client lives in one of the 24 (close-call states).
Next move: in the 4 (toss-up states), plan for any bureau.
