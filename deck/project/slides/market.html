In Scope
Bureau pulls: Experian, Equifax and TransUnion, every state and DC.
Funding types: cards, lines, loans, SBA, auto, equipment and real estate.
Lender facts: funding type, typical score and paperwork for 204 lenders (47 low or no doc).
A sequence: an application order for seven score ranges, 500 to 800+.
Out of Scope
Approval odds: nothing here predicts approval.
Business bureaus: D&B and similar files are not mapped.
Soft pulls and rates: prequalification and live rates are left out.
Advice: nothing here is legal, tax or financial advice.
Who It Serves
Clients: personal and business credit clients.
Firms: credit consultants, funding companies, advisors and brokers.
Reviewers: analysts and partners tracing each number.
