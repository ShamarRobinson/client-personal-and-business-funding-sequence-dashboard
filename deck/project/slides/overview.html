Overview: Context, Sources and Limits

What this answers, where the data comes from, how it is updated and what it cannot tell you.
OVERVIEWMAPSDECIDE
Context
Every hard inquiry lands on one of three credit reports. The same person can have a strong report at one bureau and a weak one at another, and no lender publishes which bureau it pulls by state.
Purpose
Forecast the bureau a lender will pull in each state, then turn that into an application order for personal and business funding.
Sources and Updates
Sources: a 355-row inquiry table from 16 states plus 670 outside datapoints from 56 cited sources: Doctor of Credit tables (2008 to 2019), the myFICO state grid (2023), 2025 to 2026 forum posts, MyBankTracker, CardRight, StackEasy and lender pages.
As of: October 7, 2026.
Updates: lender minimums are reviewed every quarter; each new datapoint reruns the cleaning, maps and model from the same scripts.
Data Limits
Consumer-reported: no lender publishes its bureau by state.
Uneven in age: most pulls are from 2008 to 2016; the newest are 2025 and 2026.
Thin states: under 10 pulls is a hint, not a finding.
Not a promise: income, utilization and recent inquiries decide approvals. Not financial advice.
