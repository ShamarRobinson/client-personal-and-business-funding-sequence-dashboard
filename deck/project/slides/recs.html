Approvals That Stack Up

Personal Credit Clients

Best move: Pull all three reports, then apply where your strongest report gets pulled in your state.

Choose this when: one report is clearly stronger or cleaner.

Next move: check your lender on Tableau card 5; prequalify first.

Business Credit Clients

Best move: Treat business card applications as personal inquiries.

Choose this when: you apply for business cards as an owner.

Next move: build the business file in parallel; review the 47 (low or no doc) lenders.

Credit Consulting Companies

Best move: Plan disputes and inquiry spacing bureau by bureau.

Choose this when: a client's three reports disagree or carry errors.

Next move: fix the report target lenders pull first, then apply.

Business Funding Companies

Best move: Stack in strictest-rule order; spread each round across bureaus.

Choose this when: a client is at 700+ (credit score).

Next move: use the funding ladder and one lender per bureau group per round.

Funding Advisors and Brokers

Best move: Run the close-call check before advising.

Choose this when: the client lives in one of the 24 (close-call states).

Next move: in the 4 (toss-up states), plan for any bureau.

Every recommendation traces back to a KPI or an earlier slide. A planning guide, not financial advice.
